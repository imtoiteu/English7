Two ways to get them

All at once. From a clone of the repository, run python3 tools/fetch_audio.py. It downloads whatever is missing into audio/, skips what you already have, and is safe to re-run after a dropped connection. Then python3 tools/fetch_audio.py --verify confirms all 99 are present and readable.

One at a time. Right-click a link below and choose “Save link as…”, then save it under the exact name in the Save as column — the course looks for that filename. Tick a row to remember you have it; the ticks are stored in this browser only.

Into a download manager. Copy all URLs puts the whole list on your clipboard, one per line.
