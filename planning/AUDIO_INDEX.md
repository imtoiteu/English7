test cannot be built from material the students are about to study, so these were sourced
separately from the same provider and put through the same checks: page fetched, published
transcript extracted, audio downloaded, duration / bitrate / sample rate / channels read
with `ffprobe`, mean and peak levels measured with `ffmpeg volumedetect`, and speech rate
computed from transcript words over the measured duration.

All ten are **VOA Learning English — *Let's Learn English*, Level 1**, and that is
deliberate rather than incidental: VOA is public domain, so unlike the ELLLO half of the
course corpus these may legally be **printed inside a test paper** with credit. All ten are
MP3, 128 kbps, 44.1 kHz, stereo.

None of this audio is synthetic, and none of it was recorded by the teacher.

Re-verify at any time with:

```bash
python3 tools/check_diagnostic.py --probe
```

**"Script" column.** *full* means the whole recording is played and the whole published
transcript is printed in the teacher's book. *excerpt* means only the opening is played;
the teacher's book prints the stop cue as well as the timecode, because a cue line is more
reliable than a counter in a classroom.

**Speed spread.** The 92 teaching sessions run 97–131 words per minute. The diagnostic
deliberately reaches outside that range at both ends: `BRIDGE` at 88 wpm is the slowest
recording available anywhere in this course and is used with the Foundation group, whose
problem is usually speed rather than vocabulary; `EXT` at 153 wpm is the fastest, carries a
news-interview register and past continuous throughout, and is kept for the Extension group.
