## 2. Sources selected, with licence findings

### Tier 1 — VOA *Let's Learn English*, Level 1 (52 lessons) — **the backbone**
- learningenglish.voanews.com · lesson index mirror: https://www.manythings.org/voa/lessons/1/
- American English, professional actors (Anna, Pete…), deliberately slowed "Learning English" delivery, A1→A2.
- Each lesson: video **+** audio **+** full conversation transcript **+** pronunciation practice **+** listening quiz **+** free PDF teacher guides (docs.voanews.eu).
- **Licence — the decisive advantage:** VOA's Terms of Use states that material produced **exclusively by VOA is in the public domain**, with credit to voanews.com / Voice of America.
  ⚠️ Caveat stated in the same terms: some VOA output embeds AFP/AP/Reuters material that is **not** public domain. That applies to news reporting, not to this in-house drama series — but confirm per lesson before redistributing.
- **This is the only source we may legally copy, edit and embed in our own test papers.**

### Tier 2 — British Council LearnEnglish Teens (A1 / A2 listening)
- learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org · British English, professionally produced, **written for teenagers** — the best audience match of any source.
- Each page: audio, transcript, preparation task, and 2–3 graded exercises.
- **Licence — unclear for our use, and I will not overstate it.** British Council terms permit accessing, copying, downloading and printing content for **personal and non-commercial use** with credit; any other purpose needs prior written approval. Whether a teacher playing it to a class falls inside "personal" is genuinely ambiguous.
  → **Recommendation: link/stream only. Do not copy audio into our files.** If we want more certainty, write to the British Council; they are usually responsive to state-school requests.

### Tier 3 — ELLLO (elllo.org)
- **Verified directly.** FAQ confirms recordings are **real people** ("authentic English from real speakers"), no synthetic voices.
- **Licence:** © ELLLO productions. The FAQ explicitly says teachers **may download the audio and use it in their classrooms or on a class LMS**. Republication elsewhere is *not addressed* → treat as classroom-use-permitted, redistribution-unclear.
- Two series matter enormously to us:
  - **Sound Grammar A1** (25 lessons) and **A2** (10 lessons) — short conversations built around **one grammar point each**. These map onto our grammar syllabus almost 1:1.
  - **Views** (A2) — natural two-speaker conversations.
- Speakers are mixed native (USA/England/Australia) and non-native ("World English"). I have preferred native-speaker pairs below, except in Unit 12 where the international mix is *pedagogically the point*.

### Tier 4 — Randall's ESL Cyber Listening Lab (esl-lab.com)
- Human recordings, graded Basic / Easy / Intermediate, transcripts + quizzes.
- **Licence:** all rights reserved, **but the Terms of Use expressly permit use for classroom instruction and printing the associated pages.** Clear enough to use — link/stream, no redistribution.

### Tier 5 — Official *Tiếng Anh 7 Global Success* audio (NXB Giáo dục Việt Nam)
- https://hanhtrangso.nxbgd.vn/ebook/read/tieng-anh-7-global-success-10997 · hoclieu.vn
- The single best level and syllabus match in existence — recorded for exactly this grade and curriculum.
- **Licence: copyrighted by NXBGDVN.** Free access is provided to teachers through the publisher's own platforms. **Link only. Never redistribute, and do not use the copies circulating on vndoc/tip.edu.vn etc. — those are almost certainly unauthorised.**

### Considered and rejected as backbone
- **BBC Learning English** — output is overwhelmingly B1+, too fast and too idiomatic for Grade 7. Keep only for *pronunciation* clips.
- **Breaking News English** — human voice actor (Sean Daeley), graded levels, but news topics skew mature; supplementary only.
- **Random YouTube** — excluded per your instruction.

## 3. The structural recommendation (please decide this first)

Our current design gives **all 92 sessions a full listening text**. That was my choice when writing the course, and it is more listening than any real coursebook carries. Forcing 92 external recordings would guarantee weak matches.

**Proposal — a 3-tier listening architecture:**

| Tier | Sessions | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core listening** (Lessons 1, 5, 6) | 36 | BC Teens / VOA / Randall's | Full-length, task-rich, the real listening work |
| **Grammar-in-context** (Lessons 2, 3) | 24 | ELLLO Sound Grammar | 60–90 s, one grammar point, exactly our syllabus |
| **Functional** (Lesson 4) | 12 | VOA *Let's Learn English* | Functional language inside a drama scene |
| **Looking Back** (Lesson 7) | 12 | **Recycled** — re-use that unit's audio for a dictation/quiz | Better revision than new input; removes 12 forced matches |
| **Review & test** | 8 | **VOA only** (public domain) | We must legally embed audio in a test paper |

Result: **80 sessions with real human audio, 12 recycled** — and no forced matches.

## 6. What this costs us in course changes

Adopting real audio means genuine rewriting, not relabelling:

1. **All 92 listening scripts** in `curriculum/units/*.py` are replaced by (a) the real transcript where licence permits quoting, or (b) a source pointer + our own comprehension tasks.
2. **~165 comprehension tasks and their answer keys** must be rewritten against the real recordings.
3. **Lesson procedures** change: "Play the recording twice" becomes real, plus an access route (link, QR, offline copy where permitted).
4. **Materials lines** change from the fictional `Audio 1.1` to a real citation.
5. **Cultural cost, and you should weigh it:** we lose Vietnamese names and local content in the listening strand (Mai, Nam, banh chung, the Lim Festival). Reading, speaking and writing keep their Vietnamese context; listening becomes British/American/international. That is a real trade-off — and it is also exactly what makes it authentic listening practice.
6. **Offline reality:** if classroom internet is unreliable, only **VOA (public domain)** may be safely pre-downloaded and kept on a school laptop. BC/ELLLO/Randall's should be streamed or handled under their own terms.
